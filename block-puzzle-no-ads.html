A block puzzle game without an ad after every round

If you have played a free block puzzle game on your phone, you know the pattern: one round, one ad. Sometimes a 30-second ad you cannot skip. Sometimes an ad in the middle of a good run. It is the most common complaint in block puzzle reviews, and it is the main reason we built Gridwit the way we did.

Why most block puzzles show so many ads

Most block puzzle games are free, and the simplest way to earn money from a free game is to show a full-screen ad as often as possible. That works for the developer in the short run, but it breaks the game. A block puzzle is all about flow: one more piece, one more clear, one more round. An ad every two minutes kills that feeling.

How ads work in Gridwit

Gridwit is free, and it does have ads. We think it is better to be honest about that. The difference is how they are used:

  • No ad after every round. You can finish a game and start the next one without sitting through an ad each time.
  • Rewarded ads are your choice. Some ads give you something, like a revive. You only watch them if you want the reward. Revive is limited to once per game, so the game stays fair.
  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase. If you never want to see an ad, buy Remove Ads once and that is it. No subscription, no monthly fee.

What you get instead

Fewer interruptions is only half of it. Gridwit also fixes the other big complaint about block puzzles: unfair pieces. Gridwit is designed to avoid hands that cannot fit on your board, so when a game ends, it is because of your moves, not an impossible piece sequence.

And instead of one mode, you get six:

  • Classic: the timeless 8x8 block puzzle with combos, PERFECT clears and a ranking.
  • Time Rush: 60 seconds on the clock, every clear adds time.
  • Starlight: turn seeds into stars and charge a Super Nova.
  • Adventure: 54 hand-built levels across 3 worlds, with goals and 3 stars.
  • Daily Puzzle: a new puzzle every day, always solvable.
  • Zen: no timer and no pressure.

Fewer ads, more playing

A good block puzzle should let you play. If you are tired of games that show you more ads than puzzles, give Gridwit a try. And if you still find an ad annoying, tell us at support@gridwit.app. We read every message.

Try it on Gridwit

Gridwit is a free 8x8 block puzzle for Android with six modes: Classic, Time Rush, Starlight, Adventure (54 levels), Daily Puzzle and Zen. Fair pieces, big combos, a ranking and far fewer ads than most block puzzles.

Questions

Is there a block puzzle game without ads?

Gridwit is free and does show some ads, but there is no ad after every round, rewarded ads are optional, and a one-time Remove Ads purchase removes ads completely, with no subscription.

Is Remove Ads in Gridwit a subscription?

No. Remove Ads is a single one-time purchase on Google Play.

Do I have to watch ads to keep playing?

No. Rewarded ads, like a revive, are optional. You can always start a new game without watching one.
